# Falling-behind & copy-paste alert spec — 2026-05-12

Follow-up to [ux-locked-5-12-2026.md](ux-locked-5-12-2026.md). Captures the alert system Jack and Claude designed on 2026-05-12 for surfacing struggling students to the instructor + TA.

Alerts are emailed to **Jack + the instructor only — never to students.** This preserves the "human touch" mandate from the [5/11 meeting](summary-5-11-2026.md): the tool surfaces signals, the outreach is always done by a person.

---

## When alerts fire

Two emails per week, both as digests:

1. **Mid-week check** — recommended Wednesday morning. Gives the team time to reach out Wed–Sat while the student can still recover.
2. **End-of-week summary** — Sunday night, after the week closes. Final state.

Empty sections are omitted; an email with no flagged students and no improvements isn't sent at all.

---

## Flag types

### 🔴 Red

Fires when `problems_solved_this_week ≤ 2`.

### 🟡 Yellow

Fires when `problems_solved_this_week < 0.5 × (recommended_count + choice_count)`.

The numerator counts *any* problem the student solved that week, not just from the assigned lists. A student who does 8 random LeetCode problems but zero recommended ones is fine — the philosophy is *"is this student practicing?"* rather than *"did they hit the exact list?"*

### 📋 Copy-paste flag

Fires when more than half of the student's successful submissions this week involved any paste event during the session.

No code-percentage threshold — the flag means "go check the keystroke playback to judge intent." Pasting a single import vs. pasting an entire solution both trigger the flag; humans decide.

### Suppression rules

- **Red supersedes yellow.** A student who would qualify for both appears only in the red section, never both. Avoids double-emailing the worst cases.
- **Copy-paste is independent.** It can fire alongside red, yellow, or with no other flag.

## Email format

```
Subject: CS 393 alert — week 6, mid-week check
To: jack684@byu.edu, instructor@byu.edu

🔴 Red (≤2 problems solved this week)
  • Andrew R.    — 3rd week in red
  • Maria C.     — 1st week in red

🟡 Yellow (<50% of assigned)
  • Bryce W.     — 2nd week in yellow
  • Sarah K.     — 1st week in yellow

📋 Copy-paste flags (>half of solutions pasted)
  • Andrew R.    — 2nd week flagged
  • Sarah K.     — 1st week flagged

↗ Improving since last week
  • Joe T.       — was yellow, now clear
  • Maria C.     — was red, now yellow (still flagged)
```

---

## Streak counting

The "Nth week in X" annotation **resets when the flag category changes.**

- A student red last week → yellow this week shows as **1st week in yellow**, not 2nd week flagged.
- A student yellow for 3 consecutive weeks shows "3rd week in yellow."
- Streaks are per-category: red, yellow, and copy-paste each have their own counters.

Rationale: this surfaces the *direction* of struggle. A student moving red → yellow → clear is responding to outreach; treating that as "3 weeks flagged" would obscure the improvement.

---

## "Improving" section

Lists anyone whose state moved *upward* between the previous email and this one. Two upward moves count:

- **Flagged → clear** ("was yellow, now clear")
- **Red → yellow** ("was red, now yellow — still flagged")

This lets the team see who's responding to outreach even when they're still flagged. Without it, the email is doom-only.

Downward moves (clear → yellow, yellow → red) don't get their own section — they show up in the red/yellow lists as new entries (1st week in that category).

## Open implementation questions

- **Exact mid-week timing.** Wednesday morning is the recommendation. Could be Tuesday afternoon if you want earlier intervention windows.
- **Week boundary.** When does a "week" close — Sunday night, Saturday, or course-calendar-defined? Affects when end-of-week fires and how `problems_solved_this_week` is bucketed.
- **Streak storage.** Store streak counters in Firestore as a separate field, or derive from the activity log at email-send time? Either works; derive-on-send is simpler but slower for large classes.
- **Email pipeline.** The instructor mentioned a pre-existing BYU Pathway email pipeline (referenced in [ux-5-11-2026.md](ux-5-11-2026.md#related-infrastructure-to-reuse-future)) — reuse that pattern rather than building from scratch.
- **Scaling to more TAs.** Currently "Jack + instructor." When the class has multiple TAs, do all TAs see all flags, or does each TA see a roster slice?
