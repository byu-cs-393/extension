CS 393 Buddy — Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 August 2026

CS 393 Buddy is a Chrome extension built for one course: BYU CS 393, Advanced Algorithms and Problem Solving. It is used only by students enrolled in that course, and it exists to track coursework and submit it to Canvas on the student's behalf.

The short version. On LeetCode problem pages, the extension records what you type in the code editor, what you paste and copy, and when the tab is in the foreground. Your instructor and TAs can see it. It records nothing on any other website.

What is collected

DataWhenWhy
Your BYU netID and name During onboarding, read from your signed-in Canvas session To identify you to the course and to submit work as you
Your LeetCode username and solved problems While signed in to LeetCode To show weekly progress and fill in assignment submissions
Editor keystrokes — every insertion and deletion in the LeetCode code editor, with timestamps Only on leetcode.com/problems/… pages So a TA can replay how a solution came together, and to measure time actually spent working
Clipboard contents — text you paste or copy on those pages, including the text itself Only on leetcode.com/problems/… pages Academic-integrity review by course staff
Tab focus changes Only on leetcode.com/problems/… pages So time with the tab in the background isn't counted as study time
Assignment progress and Canvas submissions When you request a signoff or submit To record coursework and avoid duplicate submissions

What is not collected

  • Anything on websites other than LeetCode problem pages and BYU Canvas. The extension has no access to your other tabs, browsing history, or clipboard elsewhere.
  • Your Canvas or LeetCode password. The extension never sees either.
  • Anything while the extension is removed or disabled.

Who can see it

The course instructor and teaching assistants for CS 393. Nobody else. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train anything.

Recorded sessions are visible to course staff in a TA dashboard, which shows time spent, typing patterns, and a replay of the editor. Where the extension highlights something for review, it presents it as an observation with its most likely innocent explanation attached — it does not accuse anyone of anything, and a human always decides what a session means.

Where it is stored

In Google Cloud Firestore, in the project cs393-496021, controlled by the course instructor. Access is enforced by security rules: a student can read and write only their own records; course staff can read all of them. Submitted coursework is also stored in BYU Canvas, under the same policies as any other assignment you submit.

A small amount of data is cached locally in your browser (chrome.storage) so pages load quickly. Removing the extension clears it.

How long it is kept

For the duration of the course and while grades may be appealed. Course records may be retained afterwards under BYU's academic record policies. You can ask the instructor to delete your recorded sessions at any time; that request will be honoured unless the data is part of an open academic integrity matter.

Your choices

Using the extension is a course requirement, but it is entirely within your control while installed:

  • A badge appears on every LeetCode problem page showing whether recording is active. It is always visible while recording.
  • Removing the extension at chrome://extensions stops all collection immediately.
  • Recording only happens on LeetCode problem pages. Closing that tab ends it.

Changes

If what the extension collects changes, this page is updated and students are told through the course. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
