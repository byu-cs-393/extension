Welcome to CS 393 Buddy

Setup takes about 2 minutes.

What this does
  • Tracks your weekly LeetCode work automatically
  • Reports your grade to Canvas
  • Lets you request topic exam signoffs from a TA
What it accesses
  • Your BYU Canvas session (to verify you're in CS 393)
  • Your LeetCode profile + solve history
  • What you type in the LeetCode editor, while you're on a problem page — see below
About editor recording

On LeetCode problem pages, the extension records your editing session: the keystrokes you make in the code editor, text you paste or copy, and when you switch away from the tab. A red ● CS 393 recording badge is visible on the page whenever this is happening.

  • Instructors and TAs can see how long you actively worked, and can replay a session to watch how your solution took shape
  • It's used to give you credit for the work you put in, and to support academic-honesty review
  • Recording only happens on leetcode.com/problems/… — nothing is captured on any other site or tab
What we don't do
  • Read anything unrelated to CS 393
  • Record any page other than LeetCode problem pages
  • Share your data with anyone outside the class

Help

Common issues
Canvas not detected

Make sure you're signed in at byu.instructure.com and that the tab has finished loading, then click Re-check session.

"Not enrolled" error

If you just enrolled, Canvas can take a few hours to sync. Try again later, or contact your instructor.

Wrong Canvas account signed in

The Canvas account you're signed in with must be your own BYU account. Use the Sign in to Canvas with a different account link.

LeetCode not detected

Sign in at leetcode.com in another tab and complete the email verification if prompted, then click Re-check session.

Still stuck?

Email jack684@byu.edu with a screenshot of what you're seeing.
