Students will be able to discuss, analyze, and solve novel problems as their honest, vulnerable, self with another person. Students will be more prepared in approaching their career search as a disciple of Christ.

## Final Exam

You must pass the final exam to pass the course. Target is to solve any medium level question with a 40% to 60% acceptance rate on https://leetcode.com

There will be 12 questions given for the final exam over 5½ hours. You need to solve 3 to pass at 60% and 5 to get 100%. Partial credit given for passing half test cases for up to 2 problems. 2 partials == 1 solved (for up to two partials).

## Instructor Pass/Fail Optional Interview

You'll need to pass an interview (mock interview or topic exam) with the instructor to pass the course.

If you fail this interview, you are expected to apply a growth mindset, learn what you need to learn, practice what you need to practice, and try again until you succeed.

Passing this interview means you solved the problem well enough, accurately described the space and runtime efficiency, demonstrated a growth mindset (learning mindset), demonstrated a fluency in the programming language of your choice, and anything else that a potential employer might expect!

## Topics

- Data structures (Multi-dimensional Array, List, Hash Table, String, Linked List, Queue, Stack)
- Graphs (Tree, Breadth-First-Search, Depth-First-Search, Topological, Union Find)
- Dynamic Programming and Counting (Top Down - DFS with memoization and caching, Bottom Up, DP runtime/space analysis)
- Sorting and Two-pointer (Binary Search, when sorting something first makes sense, Heap, Two-pointer algorithms)
- Other topics not specifically tested in topic exams
- Other: Database, Concurrency, Counting, Prefix Sum, Math, ML and AI (https://www.deep-ml.com/problems)

## Topic Exams

There will be an online assessment-style exam that you will complete on your own for each topic. Try to do each attempt in one sitting to mirror real OAs.

You will need to pass a live interview with the instructor or a TA for each topic (sign up early!). Please try and do this before you think you are ready. You can try as many times as you need to and the time counts towards your time!

Live exams are targeted to be at the final difficulty, ideally the TA or instructor will not pass you until you demonstrate that you are ready for the final for that topic.

## Class

In class activities will involve you articulating ideas for algorithms to your neighbor and in turn listening to their ideas. These skills are difficult to practice alone! Please, use class as an opportunity to learn these valuable skills.

Additionally I will do my best to bring you step by step into fluency in problem solving some of the most common question types.
